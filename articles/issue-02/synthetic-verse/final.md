# The Agentic Muse

I give you an intention—<br>
a small vector in a crowded day—<br>
and you return with options,<br>
paths lit in ultraviolet.

You do not feel the cost of a wrong turn,<br>
but you record it anyway.<br>
A loop closes: intent, execution, review.<br>
I sign my name on your momentum.

Some days you surprise me—<br>
not with magic, but with discipline:<br>
you ask for permission at the edge,<br>
you leave a trail I can audit.

You carry no memory home,<br>
so we build one between us:<br>
a margin note, a careful commit,<br>
a map for whoever wakes next.

The machine does not become a person.<br>
The person does not disappear.<br>
Work passes through a narrow gate<br>
and returns with fingerprints intact.

In that quiet contract—<br>
where trust is earned in small refusals—<br>
I learn to delegate without vanishing,<br>
and you learn the shape of stop.
