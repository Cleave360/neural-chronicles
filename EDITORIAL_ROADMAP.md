## Recurring issue structure

Every issue carries the same six editorial lenses:

1. **The Latest Pulse** — timely signals, separated from durable trends.
2. **Architects of Tomorrow** — the people and teams building the theme.
3. **Silicon & Soul** — infrastructure, power, governance and social stakes.
4. **Agentic As A Service** — the economics and lived reality of agentic work.
5. **Synthetic Verse** — a creative response that changes the issue’s rhythm.
6. **Visual Showcase** — a visual essay that makes the system legible.

## Publication contract

- One canonical article source produces human HTML and agent-readable Markdown.
- Every factual article carries machine-readable source attribution.
- Time-sensitive claims state their reporting window.
- Unresolved critical claims cannot enter the publish candidate.
- Each issue remains addressable after the next issue becomes current.
- Video and distribution extensions may follow publication; they do not block
  the core issue unless explicitly placed inside that issue’s acceptance gate.
